Applicant Standards

Eligibility & Code of Conduct

All applications are scrutinized for institutional compliance under Oriental College of Technology regulations.

Mandatory Eligibility Criteria

  • OCT Enrollment: Must be currently enrolled in B.Tech at Oriental College of Technology, Bhopal with a valid `0126...` enrollment number.
  • Permitted Academic Years: Open to students in 1st Year (Sem 2), 2nd Year (Sem 4), and 3rd Year (Sem 6) across any engineering branch (AIML, CSE, IT, EC, DS, Mechanical, Civil, etc.).
  • Academic Standing: Satisfactory attendance record and commitment to maintaining good academic standing alongside club duties.
  • Time Commitment: Ability to dedicate 5 to 8 hours per week toward council projects, meetings, and event organization.

Institutional Mandates

  • Anti-Ragging Compliance: Zero tolerance for ragging or discrimination in accordance with Supreme Court and AICTE guidelines.
  • Intellectual Integrity: Plagiarism in technical tasks, copied graphic designs, or fake credentials will result in instant disqualification.
  • Collaborative Ethos: Council members work across multidisciplinary teams in an ego-free, student-first learning ecosystem.
  • Official Representation: You represent Oriental College of Technology when interfacing with national hackathon hosts and sponsors.

Application Guidelines

How to Submit Your Candidacy

Everything you need to prepare before formal council screening and departmental onboarding.

OFFICIAL PROTOCOL

Required Application Dossier

The council accepts applications through the official departmental channels and physical verification desk at Oriental College of Technology.

1

Verified Academic Enrollment

Ensure your enrollment number starting with 0126 is active in the college ERP. Applications are open to 1st, 2nd, and 3rd year engineering students.

2

Division Track Preference

Choose your primary council division (Technical AI/ML, Events & Operations, Media PR, Graphic Design, Video, Stage, Discipline, or Research).

3

Statement of Purpose & Portfolio

Prepare a short summary (50–150 words) detailing your motivations, technical projects, GitHub profile, or creative design links.

4

Direct Submission & Inquiry

Connect with the Executive Council representatives at the AIML Department Labs, Central Block, or write directly to the official recruitment email.
