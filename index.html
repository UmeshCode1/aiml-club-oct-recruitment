Don't use AI to write your answers. Answer in Hindi, English or Hinglish — whatever feels natural to you. Be original. We're looking for real thinking and the mindset of future leaders, not perfect or synthetic language.

Submitting an application does not guarantee selection. Applicants may be contacted for profile screening, an interview, or a practical challenge task.
