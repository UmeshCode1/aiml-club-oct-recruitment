1. Application: Multi-Dimensional Profile

Candidates submit their technical domains, past projects, skill proficiencies, and aspirations. The portal ensures immediate receipt logging with zero friction.

🔐 SECURE VERIFICATION STANDARD • DIRECTLY IN EMAIL

Unique QR + Application PDF Delivered Directly to Candidate Email

Every applicant receives an official confirmation email containing their unique, encrypted QR code and downloadable Application Record PDF. During screening rounds, Core Team members scan this QR code directly from the candidate's phone or email for instantaneous, paperless verification.

✓ Unique QR in confirmation email ✓ 1-second record lookup ✓ Downloadable PDF docket ✓ Anti-tamper verification ✓ Zero paper waste
